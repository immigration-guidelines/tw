Taiwan (tw) - notes on sources, checked 15 November 2026

Authorities: National Immigration Agency (內政部移民署, NIA) for entry, residence, permanent residence,
mainland/Hong Kong/Macau visitors, detention and removal; Bureau of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of
Foreign Affairs (外交部領事事務局) for visas; Workforce Development Agency of the Ministry of Labor
(勞動部勞動力發展署, WDA) for work permits of foreign professionals.

USED (all robots.txt read first; see download_tw.py for the exact pages)
1. NIA, www.immigration.gov.tw (robots.txt bars only the ZoomEye bot)
   - 入出國及移民法令彙編, the agency's compendium of statutes, regulations, administrative rules
     (作業要點, 作業規定, 處理原則, 審查基準, 裁罰基準) and interpretive letters, every edition offered:
     Dec 2014, Nov 2015, Nov 2016, Nov 2017, Jan 2019, Apr 2020, Nov 2021, Oct 2022, Jul 2024, Aug 2026
     (10 PDFs, 1,600-1,700 pp. each, 73 MB). Mixed content (it also prints the statutes), kept as
     guidance because it is the agency's own working compilation and the only reachable full text of
     most of its administrative rules. https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5385/7353/7356/
   - 主管法規 pages (https://www.immigration.gov.tw/5385/396729/): 11 administrative rules and notices
     the agency hosts itself as files (Hong Kong/Macau entry and residence operating rules, biometric
     and e-gate operating directions, mainland tourism quotas and procedures, interpretive orders).
2. WDA, ezworktaiwan.wda.gov.tw (robots.txt 404; files on ws.wda.gov.tw, robots.txt 404)
   - 審查作業手冊: 10 review manuals for work permits (specialised/technical work, managers, cram-school
     teachers, sports, arts, contract personnel, foreign graduates points system, student part-time work,
     termination, the Foreign Professionals Act). Dated by the page's "updated" date (2025-04-30); the
     files themselves carry their own edition dates in their names.
   - 會商機制: 12 files on the case-by-case consultation mechanism (legal basis, procedure, flowcharts).
   - 公告及解釋令: 8 interpretive orders and review directions (2017-2025), 4 of them via
     gazette.nat.gov.tw (robots.txt 404).
3. MOFA law database, law.mofa.gov.tw (robots.txt 404), consular category: 3 rules on visas and visa
   interviews (marriage-interview operation directions = guidance; enforcement rules of the visa statute
   and employment-seeking visa regulations = procedural act), each in Chinese and official English.
4. National law database, law.moj.gov.tw. robots.txt: "Disallow: /" except /LawClass/LawAll.aspx,
   /News/NewsDetail.aspx and the sitemap. 34 regulations (法規命令) that are the working rules for
   residence and permanent residence of foreigners, mainland/HK/Macau residents, nationals without
   household registration, interviews, detention, deportation, trafficking victims and work-permit review
   standards. Marked procedural act. Found from the NIA pages and, once, by a sweep of the database's
   sitemap (Ministry of the Interior class D0080 and cross-strait classes Q001/Q002/Q006, about 260
   pages, immigration titles only); the finds are pinned in MOJ_EXTRA so re-runs need no sweep.
5. Internet Archive: 5 administrative rules of glrs.moi.gov.tw as captured 2018-2022 (fines standards,
   bar on entry of foreigners, re-application bar periods, an interpretive order, nationals' entry and
   exit). Dates are capture dates; later versions may be in force.

NOT OBTAINED (see not-obtained.tsv, 40 entries)
- glrs.moi.gov.tw, the Ministry of the Interior's own database where the NIA's administrative rules
  live (移民 category: 154 entries, 91 of them 行政規則). It timed out from this machine for the earlier
  agent and again for me (robots.txt, 40 s), so it was never used. 30-odd rules the NIA pages link
  there are listed individually; their text is printed in the compendium editions.
- laws.mol.gov.tw and law.mac.gov.tw: timed out for the earlier agent; not retried.
- law.moj.gov.tw /ENG/ English translations: excluded by robots.txt.
- Three Internet Archive captures (one failed with a refused connection, two added later) were not
  fetched because the archive refused this machine later in the task (HTTP 403, connection refusals)
  and was given up.
- A visa-processing manual of the Bureau of Consular Affairs is not published.

How much real guidance: a good deal. Taiwan publishes its administrative rules as such, and the NIA's
compendium carries nearly all of them, in ten editions since 2014. The WDA publishes its full review
manuals for work permits of foreign professionals. What is missing is the clean per-rule text from
glrs.moi.gov.tw, which this machine cannot reach, and anything on unpublished internal instructions.

Earlier work versus mine: an earlier agent wrote download_tw.py and explored the sites (notes in its
scratch folder) but had not run it; tw/ did not exist. I ran it, then added the law-database sitemap
sweep (and removed 33 police regulations a first, unfiltered sweep had saved), two Internet Archive
items, glosses, a fix for "移至" title prefixes in gazette file names and Unicode normalisation for the
gloss lookup.
No HTTP 403/429, CAPTCHA or login wall was met on any Taiwanese host. No file is over 95 MB.
