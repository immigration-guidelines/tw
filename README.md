# Immigration guidance: Taiwan

Published guidance that the authorities of Taiwan issue to the officials who decide immigration cases, collected in November 2026.

- **Guidance:** 99 files in this folder. `index.tsv` lists each file with its title, date and source address.
- The files are unmodified copies of what the source published. Where a source refused automated access, copies come from a public web archive; the index says which.
- What is missing, and how to obtain it, is described in `NOTES.txt` and `not-obtained.tsv` here, and for all jurisdictions in [MISSING.md](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines/.github/blob/main/MISSING.md), in the repository that also holds the download scripts. Other jurisdictions are listed on the [organisation page](https://github.com/immigration-guidelines).

Taiwan publishes the most of any East Asian jurisdiction collected. This repository holds all ten editions (2014 to 2026) of the National Immigration Agency's compendium of laws, administrative rules and interpretive letters (入出國及移民法令彙編, about 1,650 pages each), administrative rules the agency hosts, the Workforce Development Agency's work-permit review manuals and interpretive orders, the foreign ministry's visa rules in Chinese and English, and 34 regulations from the national law database marked `procedural act`.

The documents remain the work of the authorities that published them.
